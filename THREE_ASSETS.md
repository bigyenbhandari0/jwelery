# Diamond atelier

Model: diamond-shader-lab by Ehsan Moradi, MIT.
Source: https://github.com/ehsanwwe/diamond-shader-lab/tree/edd718e34c081d3ea0f6c99017b87cf8d022f329
License: public/licenses/diamond-ring-MIT.txt. Model is loaded from this pinned source.

The interactive model is a round-diamond halo design study, not the pink heart ring shown in the hero photo. Three.js PBR materials, drag rotation, finish selection, auto-spin and reset are provided. WebGL failure leaves the photo experience accessible. GPU appearance has not been verified in the authoring browser, where WebGL is disabled.
